Storefront

This Angular app is the public site. It talks only to cursor-node-api and must stay mobile-friendly.

  • Catalog, cart, checkout, and CMS wait for the Website module.
  • Shopper identity is separate from staff login.
  • Tenant is resolved by host or store key on the server, not by a shopper-supplied company id.

Loading, empty, error, and validation states are required on every future screen.
